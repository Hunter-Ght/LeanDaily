Steps to test (about one minute):
1. Open the Log tab > Food > Photo meal log.

2. Guidelines 5.1.1(i) and 5.1.2(i) – Sharing data with a third-party AI service

AI recognition is optional and off by default. Manual meal logging and all training features work without it.

Before EVERY photo upload, the app shows a consent screen that:
- Names the recipient: OpenAI, at https://api.openai.com/v1.
- Lists exactly what is sent: the selected meal photos, meal name, an optional note (up to 300 characters) and the nutrition estimation instructions, plus the API key for authentication and the device's IP address.
- States what is NOT sent: body profile, body measurements and workout history.
- Links to OpenAI's data-usage policy.
- Sends nothing unless the user taps "Agree & recognize". Cancel sends nothing. Granting camera or photo-library access is not treated as consent.

Consent given in earlier versions no longer applies; every user sees the new consent screen again.

On iOS, OpenAI is the only AI recipient. Custom or other third-party endpoints cannot be used, and older custom settings are never redirected to another service. Requests are sent directly from the device to OpenAI with store=false; we do not operate any server that receives this data.

Our privacy policy (also available inside the app) now states what data is collected, how it is collected, how it is used, that it is shared with OpenAI, and confirms that OpenAI provides the same or equal protection of this data, including OpenAI's retention and no-training-by-default terms:
https://hunter-ght.github.io/LeanDaily/privacy/

We have also added this information to the App Review Information notes.
