Help & support

LeanDaily is an offline-first workout and food journal for iOS and Android.

Get help

Report a problem or ask a question: GitHub Issues . Include your app version, device, OS version and steps to reproduce. Never include API keys, health records, photos or backup files. Reading issues is public; submitting one requires a GitHub account.

Support and privacy questions: 2436759690@qq.com. No GitHub account is required. Include your app version, device, OS version and steps to reproduce. Do not email API keys, full backups or unnecessary health information.

Common questions

Language: Me → Language → System, English, Simplified Chinese or Traditional Chinese.

Data: there is no account or automatic sync. Export a backup before changing phones or deleting the app.

Photos: manual meal logging works without AI. Recognition requires your own provider settings, key and explicit permission. Revoke permission in Backup & advanced.

Notifications: allow notifications in system settings. Focus mode and battery restrictions may delay reminders.

Updates: use the App Store or TestFlight on iOS. On Android, use the project release channel.

Fitness estimates are general references, not medical care. If you experience pain or illness, stop exercising and seek professional advice.

Optional OpenAI example: https://api.openai.com/v1 · gpt-6-luna · Responses. Supply your own API key; API billing is separate from ChatGPT. Filling the example does not send data. Read the privacy policy before recognizing photos.

Publisher and operator: Skylance LLC.

Google Gemini example

The optional Google Gemini example uses https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com/v1beta/openai/, gemini-3.5-flash-lite and Chat Completions. It includes no key and sends nothing until you save, consent and request recognition. Only this exact HTTPS host and compatible path is identified as Google Gemini. Free quota depends on the account, region and current limits; it is not a promise of unlimited or permanent free access. Under Google’s unpaid-service terms, inputs and outputs may be used to improve products and models and may be reviewed by humans. Do not submit sensitive, confidential or personal information, faces, children’s photos, medical history or health conditions. Paid services and certain regions have different terms. Google requires API users to be 18 or older; the app blocks official Gemini recognition when the local profile age is missing or under 18. Read the current terms and handle provider data or deletion requests directly with Google: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms . Only submit ordinary food photos and non-sensitive notes; manual meal logging remains available. This app is independent and is not endorsed by Google.
